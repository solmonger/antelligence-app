## 2. Assumptions

- **[A1]** Pod: one pod serves all three models at the rate above. v4.1 also ran d1 on the pod (hardware L40S in `decisions.jsonl`).
- **[A2]** Plan size: 80 episodes per arm (40 dev cases x 2 seeds, as in verdict1). The Phase 1 plan is not fixed; scale linearly (pod-hours scale in steps of 16 episodes).
- **[A3]** One model call per ant per decision turn; the four questions (target, release, amount, mark) share the call, as in v4.1. The `mark` question adds about one output token. A skipped turn (quiet rule) makes no call.
- **[A4]** An *ask* costs no call: the trigger is computed from the asker's own answer. An *answer* is one `noul` call to the receiver (only if it has a reading within 6 mm; otherwise `unknown` and no call, which this projection ignores: it counts every answered ask as a call, an upper bound).
- **[A5]** Model ants ask and answer as often as the scripted ants (`S-talk`: asks sent / answered above). This is the weakest assumption: the real rate depends on the thresholds fitted in Phase 1. Section 5 gives 0.5x, 2x and the budget ceiling.
- **[A6]** Quiet rule: model ants skip the same 4.3% of turns the scripted ants skip in `S-marks-quiet`; asks and answers fall in proportion.
- **[A7]** Time model. The calls of one round are started together on one server and finish close together: in v4.1 step 7 the slowest 9B call of a round was only 1.057 x the mean 9B call (round ratio above), far below what 8 independent draws would give. Headline: round time = ratio x the mean call of the slowest model in the colony (assumes the same ratio holds for d1 and 4B, which step 7 cannot show, and for a pod less loaded than in step 7: Phase 1 makes 1 call per ant turn, step 7 made 3). Section 4b gives the opposite bound, the exact expected maximum of independent draws. Prompts are longer (state about 280 against 140 tokens); prefill time is assumed negligible.
- **[A8]** An answer call takes as long as a decision call of the same model; the answers of one round are batched (one round-ratio wait). A round has an answer phase with probability 1 - exp(-answers / 24) (answers spread over rounds at random).
- **[A9]** Escalation (M-escalate): an unsure turn (probability = the unsure share above) makes a second call to the next bigger model, in sequence after the first (d1 to 4B, 4B to 9B, 9B to frontier); no sharing. A round has one such second call with probability 1 - (1 - u)^c for c ants on that model.
- **[A10]** A frontier call costs what the one live v4.1 frontier episode cost per call and a frontier round takes that episode's wall / 24 = 15.9 s. A queen call takes one frontier round. The prompts in the colony are shorter than FW's (about 1,130 tokens per call there), so the dollars are probably high.
- **[A11]** Episodes run together 16 at a time in one process (C0); a batch takes wall / efficiency, and the pod bills for the whole batch.
- **[A12]** The scripted queen and the engine cost the C0 engine time only; the LLM queen costs 4 frontier calls (wall) and the pilot's $0.004.
- **[A13]** Tokenizer counts: state p50/p95/max are in `token_counts.json`; they do not enter this table.

### Worked example: M-talk, Qwen3.5-9B colony, quiet off

1. Decision round = round ratio x mean 9B call = 1.057 x 14.10 s = 14.90 s.
2. 24 rounds x 14.90 s = 357.7 s.
3. Answer phase: 63.4 answers / 24 rounds = 2.64 per round, so a round has one with probability 1 - exp(-2.64) = 0.929; 24 x 0.929 x 1.057 x 14.10 s = 332.2 s.
4. Engine 1.7 s. Episode wall = 357.7 + 332.2 + 1.7 = 691.5 s.
5. 80 episodes at 16 together = 5 batches, each 691.5 / 0.900 = 768.5 s; pod-hours = 5 x 768.5 / 3600 = 1.067 h; x $1.09 = $1.16.

Check against v4.1: M-solo in a 9B colony projects 359 s per episode. v4.1 step 7 measured a mean wall of 479 s per episode, of which 379 s was model wait on the critical path (all three models called each turn) and 101 s was unlogged (d1 looks, engine); v4.2 has no d1 look calls, so the projection should sit near the 379 s, not near 479 s.

## 6. What to take from it

- Wall time is set by the slowest model in each round, so it moves with the model (9B round 14.9 s, 4B 12.3 s, d1 8.3 s) and barely with the number of calls. The quiet rule cuts calls by about 4% and wall time by a small fraction of that (a round still waits for its slowest call).
- Talking adds the answer turns, which are sequential with the decision rounds: that is where M-talk pays in wall time, not in dollars.
- Nous dollars are zero everywhere except the LLM queen, M-escalate in a 9B colony (escalates to frontier), and the frontier reference.
- A round waits for its slowest call, so the quiet rule (which removes calls, not rounds) barely moves wall time; it moves calls, tokens and dollars.
- The pod bills in batches of 16: 80 episodes is 5 batches, so a 17th episode costs a whole extra batch.
